Fluffy Jar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Summary

Fluffy Jar does not collect, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, selected jar theme), your local leaderboard scores and the display name you choose are stored only on your own device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

What we do not do

  • The app does not request internet access and does not connect to any server.
  • The app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the changes will be described here.
